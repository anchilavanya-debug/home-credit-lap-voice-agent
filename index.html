Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before starting the demo.

Is this final loan approval?

No. This is a preliminary screening demonstration. A qualified loan specialist must review the case and confirm any actual offer.

What is the maximum amount mentioned?

The demo discusses an offer of up to ₹75 lakh. Any actual amount depends on the lender’s assessment and applicable terms.

Can I ask for a callback?

Yes. Tell the assistant that you are busy and prefer a callback. It should collect your preferred callback time and end the current conversation.

What if I have an existing property loan?

Tell the assistant if you need a loan transfer or EMI-related assistance, so it can route the request to the appropriate specialist.
